    Wiltons

    55 Jermyn Street, St James's, London SW1Y 6LX

    Historic dining room · since 1742

    Began as an oyster stall on Haymarket in 1742 and has been on Jermyn Street since 1984. A smart, old-fashioned dining room for oysters, Dover sole and game in season. Known for: Oysters, Dover sole, Fish pie, Stilton soufflé.

    Stall Street, Bath BA1 1LZ

    Historic dining room · since 1795

    A grand Georgian room from 1795 that Jane Austen wrote into her novels. Have afternoon tea to live music from the Pump Room Trio, and taste the warm spa water from the fountain. Known for: Jane Austen Afternoon Tea, Bath bun, Spa water tasting.

    Rules

    34-35 Maiden Lane, Covent Garden, London WC2E 7LB

    Historic dining room · since 1798

    London's oldest restaurant, opened by Thomas Rule as an oyster bar in 1798. It is famous for British game, pies and puddings. Dickens, Chaplin and Graham Greene all ate here, and in 2026 the building was given to the National Trust. Known for: Seasonal game, Steak and kidney pie, Oysters, Sticky toffee pudding.

    Edinburgh's original oyster bar, founded in 1826 and 200 years old in 2026. The 1861 building is famous for its Doulton tile pictures and stained-glass windows. Known for: Scottish rock oysters, Seafood platter, Cullen skink.
