What TableFifty is

TableFifty puts the UK's half-price dining, takeaway, surplus food and grocery deals in one place. We check First Table, EatClub, TheFork, Code, tastecard, Gourmet Society, OpenTable, Too Good To Go, Deliveroo, Uber Eats, Just Eat and the big chains, then link you straight to each offer. TableFifty is not linked to, or approved by, any of these platforms or restaurants.

How we make money

  • Some links on TableFifty are affiliate links. If you click one and then book or order, the platform may pay us a small commission.
  • Your price stays the same. The commission comes from the platform, not from you.
  • Links that earn us money carry an Ad tag.
  • We list deals because they are good for you, not because they pay us.

Privacy and cookies

  • Accounts are optional. You can use TableFifty without one. If you join (with email or Google), we store only your email, your saved places, your home postcode and your deal-alert choice. Sign-in is run by Google Firebase. Your login details are held by Google, which may store them outside the UK under standard data protection safeguards. Your saved places and postcode are stored in London. You can ask us to delete your account at any time.
  • Your postcode and location are only used to sort deals by distance. A postcode or town you type is looked up with postcodes.io, a free UK postcode service. "Use my location" works inside your browser. Without an account, saved places and your postcode stay in your own browser and never reach us.
  • Food diary (optional): if you save a visit, we store the restaurant, date, your stars, best dish, notes and up to 4 photos in your account (Google Firebase). Only you can see it. You can edit or delete any visit, and deleting your account deletes the diary. Stars you choose to add to the TableFifty Score count like a normal rating.
  • Likes and ratings: when you like or rate a place, we store that choice with a random account ID so each person counts once. Like counts are public; who liked what is not.
  • Visit emails are off unless you switch them on. If you tick "email me to ask how my visit was", we keep a list of the deals you open for 30 days, so we can ask how it went. Deal alerts are also off unless you switch them on. Every email has an unsubscribe link.
  • Half-Price Friday emails are off unless you sign up. If you sign up, we keep your email, language, area and the date you said yes, stored with Google Firebase. We use it only to send the weekly deals email. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and we never sell or share your email.
  • Click counts: when you open a deal we add 1 to a monthly total for that platform (for example "First Table: 1,200 clicks in October"). It holds no personal data and sets no cookie. Links to some booking platforms carry a "tablefifty" tag so they can see we sent you.
  • Restaurant enquiry form: messages sent from our "For restaurants" page are delivered to our email by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co). We use them only to answer you, and share your details with First Table only if you tick that box.
  • We use no tracking or advertising cookies of our own right now. If we add website stats or ads later, we will update this page and ask you first.
  • Restaurant photos come from each restaurant's own First Table page and link back to it. Restaurants can ask us to change or remove a photo at hello@tablefifty.co.uk.
  • When you click through to a platform, that platform's own privacy policy applies. It may set a cookie so it knows TableFifty sent you.
  • Your rights: you can ask what we hold about you or ask us to delete it by emailing hello@tablefifty.co.uk. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

How the TableFifty Score works

  • The score is out of 10. It combines the diner ratings shown on First Table, EatClub and TheFork with star ratings from signed-in TableFifty users.
  • Places with only a few reviews start close to 8, so a 10/10 from three people never beats 9.5 from hundreds. The number next to the score is how many reviews it uses.
  • Google ratings are shown only on Google Maps, through the "Google reviews" link. They are not part of our score.
  • MICHELIN badges (Stars, Bib Gourmand, Green Star) come from the MICHELIN Guide Great Britain & Ireland 2026. MICHELIN is a trademark of Michelin; TableFifty is not linked to the guide.
  • "In their own words" quotes are the short description each restaurant publishes on its own website.
  • "Perfect for" tags (date night, special treat, hidden gem and so on) come from how each restaurant is described on the booking platforms.
  • "Today's pick" is chosen each day from top-scoring places with a live deal, favouring places with the most new diner reviews since yesterday.

Our reviews policy

  • Only signed-in users can rate a place, and each person gets one rating per place. You can change your own rating.
  • We never remove honest low ratings, and we never pay or reward anyone for a rating.
  • No restaurant can pay to change its score. If a restaurant pays for the "Spotlight" spot, it is clearly labelled Ad, and its score stays the same.
  • If you think a rating is fake, email hello@tablefifty.co.uk and we will check it.
