People who said yes to emails

Only email the list that matches what they signed up for. Every address here was confirmed by its owner (they opened our link or signed in).

How to send an email to your list

  1. Use a free email tool such as Brevo or MailerLite, not Gmail BCC. They add the unsubscribe link the law asks for and stop your Gmail being marked as spam.
  2. Download CSV for the right list above, then import it into the tool. Tick "these people gave consent".
  3. Every email must say who you are (TableFifty, info@tablefifty.co.uk) and have an unsubscribe link.
  4. When someone unsubscribes in the tool or asks you by email, press Remove next to their address here too.
  5. "All members" is only for account messages (for example a change to the privacy policy). Never send offers to that list.

Personal data on this page is protected by UK GDPR. Don't share screenshots or the CSV files, and delete old CSV files after you import them.
