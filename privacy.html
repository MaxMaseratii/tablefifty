About & privacy

What TableFifty is

TableFifty puts London's half-price dining and delivery deals in one place. We check First Table, TheFork, Tastecard, OpenTable, Deliveroo, Uber Eats and Just Eat, then link you straight to the venue's page on each one. TableFifty is not linked to, or approved by, any of these platforms or restaurants.

How we make money

  • Some links on TableFifty are affiliate links. If you click one and then book or order, the platform may pay us a small commission.
  • Your price stays the same. The commission comes from the platform, not from you.
  • Links that earn us money carry an Ad tag.
  • We list deals because they are good for you, not because they pay us.

Privacy and cookies

  • We don't ask for your name, email or card. There are no accounts.
  • Venues you save stay in your own browser. They never reach us. Clearing your browser data deletes them.
  • We use no tracking or advertising cookies of our own right now. If we add website stats or ads later, we will update this page and ask you first.
  • When you click through to a platform, that platform's own privacy policy applies. It may set a cookie so it knows TableFifty sent you.
  • Your rights: you can ask what we hold about you or ask us to delete it by emailing hello@tablefifty.co.uk. If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Who runs TableFifty

TableFifty is run by Max Maserati in England. Contact: hello@tablefifty.co.uk.
