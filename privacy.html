About & privacy

Why TableFifty exists

Eating out is not about hunger. It's about pleasure: a night with friends, a date, a treat. A good deal doesn't make it feel cheap. It makes it even better.

One night I picked a restaurant and wanted to find an offer. Was it on First Table? No. TheFork? EatClub? tastecard? I opened one app after another. It took ages.

So I built a small tool just for me: type a restaurant name and see every offer in one place. Then I thought: everyone must have this problem. So I made it free for everyone.

— Max, founder

What TableFifty is

TableFifty puts the UK's half-price dining, takeaway, surplus food and grocery deals in one place. We check First Table, EatClub, TheFork, Code, tastecard, Gourmet Society, OpenTable, Too Good To Go, Deliveroo, Uber Eats, Just Eat and the big chains, then link you straight to each offer. TableFifty is not linked to, or approved by, any of these platforms or restaurants.

How we make money

  • Some links on TableFifty are affiliate links. If you click one and then book or order, the platform may pay us a small commission.
  • Your price stays the same. The commission comes from the platform, not from you.
  • Links that earn us money carry an Ad tag.
  • We list deals because they are good for you, not because they pay us.

Privacy policy

Who we are. TableFifty (tablefifty.co.uk) is run by Max Maserati in England, who decides how your data is used (the "data controller"). Contact: info@tablefifty.co.uk.

You can use TableFifty without giving us any personal data. Everything below only applies if you choose to use the feature.

What we keep, why, and for how long

  • Account (optional): your email (or Google sign-in), first name if you add it, saved places, home postcode and your email choices. Why: to run your account (UK GDPR "contract"). Kept until you delete your account.
  • Food diary (optional): restaurant, date, stars, best dish, notes and up to 4 photos per visit. Only you can see it. Kept until you delete the visit or your account.
  • Likes and star ratings: stored with a random account ID so each person counts once. Totals are public; who liked or rated what is not. If you delete your account, your stars stay only inside the anonymous totals.
  • Half-Price Friday emails (optional): your email, language, area and the date you said yes. We only add you after you open the confirmation link we email you (double opt-in). Why: your consent. Kept until you unsubscribe.
  • Deal alerts and "how was your visit?" emails (optional): off unless you switch them on in your account. For visit emails we keep the deals you open for 30 days, then they are deleted automatically. Why: your consent.
  • Restaurant enquiries: what you send on our "For restaurants" page, delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit. Used only to reply, and shared with First Table only if you tick that box. Kept up to 2 years after our last message.
  • Visit and click counts: we add 1 to simple totals (visits per day, where visitors came from such as "Instagram", clicks per platform). No personal data, no cookie, nothing stored on your device. Why: to run and improve the site (legitimate interests).

Who helps us (processors)

  • Google Firebase runs sign-in and our database. Your saved places, diary and email choices are stored in London. Google may handle sign-in data outside the UK under the UK's approved data protection safeguards.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Like every web host, it briefly logs visitors' IP addresses for security.
  • postcodes.io turns a postcode or town you type into a map position. We send the postcode only, nothing about you.
  • FormSubmit delivers restaurant enquiry forms to our email.
  • Our email service (for Half-Price Friday emails) receives only the email addresses of people who signed up.
  • When the page opens, your browser also loads restaurant photos from First Table's and EatClub's image servers and our sign-in code from Google (gstatic.com). Like any website, those servers see your IP address. Our fonts load from tablefifty.co.uk itself.
  • We never sell your data and never share it for advertising.

Cookies and storage on your device

  • No tracking or advertising cookies.
  • Your browser keeps a few things so the site works the way you asked: your language, saved places, your postcode or location, and (if you sign in) a sign-in token. These never leave your device unless you sign in, and clearing your browser data deletes them. Because they are needed for features you choose, UK law does not require a cookie banner for them.
  • When you click through to a platform (First Table, Deliveroo, Google Maps and so on), that site's own privacy policy and cookies apply. It may know that TableFifty sent you.

Your rights

  • See or download your data: Account → Settings → Your data → Download my data.
  • Delete everything: Account → Settings → Your data → Delete my account. It's instant.
  • Stop emails: use the unsubscribe link in any email, or untick the email boxes in your account.
  • You can also ask us to correct, limit or explain the use of your data, or object to it, by emailing info@tablefifty.co.uk. We reply within one month.
  • If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk or 0303 123 1113.
  • Accounts are for people aged 13 or over.

How the TableFifty Score works

  • The score is out of 10. It combines the diner ratings shown on First Table, EatClub and TheFork with star ratings from signed-in TableFifty users.
  • Places with only a few reviews start close to 8, so a 10/10 from three people never beats 9.5 from hundreds. The number next to the score is how many reviews it uses.
  • Google ratings are shown only on Google Maps, through the "Google reviews" link. They are not part of our score.
  • MICHELIN badges (Stars, Bib Gourmand, Green Star) come from the MICHELIN Guide Great Britain & Ireland 2026. MICHELIN is a trademark of Michelin; TableFifty is not linked to the guide.
  • Short descriptions under each restaurant, and "In their own words" quotes, come from the description the restaurant publishes on its own website, or from its cuisine on First Table and EatClub. We don't invent dishes or claims.
  • Restaurant photos come from each restaurant's own First Table or EatClub page and link back to it. Restaurants can ask us to change or remove a photo or description at info@tablefifty.co.uk.
  • "Perfect for" tags (date night, special treat, hidden gem and so on) come from how each restaurant is described on the booking platforms.
  • "Today's pick" is chosen each day from top-scoring places with a live deal, favouring places with the most new diner reviews since yesterday.

Our reviews policy

  • Only signed-in users can rate a place, and each person gets one rating per place. You can change your own rating.
  • We never remove honest low ratings, and we never pay or reward anyone for a rating.
  • No restaurant can pay to change its score. If a restaurant pays for the "Spotlight" spot, it is clearly labelled Ad, and its score stays the same.
  • If you think a rating is fake, email info@tablefifty.co.uk and we will check it.
